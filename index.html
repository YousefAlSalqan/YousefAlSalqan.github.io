Ethereum transaction analyzer ↗

A command-line tool that pulls a transaction by hash from a public JSON-RPC node, decodes its ERC-20 transfer and approval events, and returns a structured risk verdict rather than a wall of hex. The hard part was deciding what counts as evidence of risk, then making the output consistent enough to act on.

  • Python
  • JSON-RPC
  • Multi-factor LLM prompting

Know-your-transaction tooling for a payments company: layered analysis of on-chain activity producing a risk assessment a compliance team can read. Same problem as above at a larger scale, and with someone else depending on the answer.

Distillation detection

A prompt-scoring pipeline for Tumeryk that spots attempts to distil a model, where the signal is spread deliberately across a whole conversation instead of sitting in any single message. An adversarial detection problem: the thing you're looking for is actively trying not to look like itself.
